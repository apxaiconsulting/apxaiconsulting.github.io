Case study · 4-cabin mountain resort

The AI audit found the numbers were wrong. Then we fixed how they're made.

The spreadsheet overstated a year of income 2.5×. The real number was less than half of what the owner was looking at.

We moved every booking into a shared app, rebuilt income and commission calculations from the bookings themselves, and gave each cabin its own calendar. The team now books, collects balances and checks profit from their phones.

Before
  • Income tab formulas pointing at the wrong cells
  • Expenses never entered, so profit was just income
  • Unpaid balances tracked in people's heads
  • Nothing stopping two bookings on the same night
●  After
  • Every total calculated from real bookings
  • Income, costs and surplus per cabin, every month
  • Balance-due list for the next two weeks
  • One calendar per cabin, double bookings blocked
  • Shared by the team, with $0 monthly hosting
